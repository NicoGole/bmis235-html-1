Hugo Ekitike

Pitch to Runway

01 · Introduction

Hugo Ekitike has had a meteoric rise in his football career, and his fashion speaks volumes.

Matchday style and everyday style have a lot in common. Both are about confidence, fit, and standing out without trying too hard. On the pitch, the kit is a uniform with personality. Off it, a clean jacket and well-chosen trousers say just as much.

Ekitike is a French striker known for his pace, movement, and finishing. The way he carries himself, on the pitch and off it, is part of the story this page tells.

04 · Style Notes

Confidence is the real accessory. On dressing like a footballer

A strong look starts with fit. Ekitike favors pieces that move with him, from relaxed trousers to structured jackets, and he isn't afraid of bold texture or color.

The result is a style that feels natural rather than staged, which is exactly what makes it work.
