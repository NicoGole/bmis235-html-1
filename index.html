Football & Fashion

Hugo Ekitike has had a meteoric rise in his football career, and his fashion speaks volumes.

Hugo Ekitike is a French striker known for his pace, movement, and finishing. He came through the French league before making his name in the Bundesliga with Eintracht Frankfurt, and he joined Liverpool in 2025.

Matchday style and everyday style have a lot in common. Both are about confidence, fit, and standing out without trying too hard. On the pitch, the kit is a uniform with personality. Off it, a clean jacket and well-chosen trousers say just as much.

Three things about Hugo Ekitike

  • He is known for his quick first step and his ability to run in behind defenders.
  • He has played in both France and Germany before his move to England.
  • He wears the Liverpool kit with the same sharp presence he brings to the pitch.
