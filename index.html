01 · Introduction

Hugo Ekitike has had a meteoric rise in his football career, and his fashion speaks volumes.

Matchday style and everyday style have a lot in common. Both are about confidence, fit, and standing out without trying too hard. On the pitch, the kit is a uniform with personality. Off it, a clean jacket and well-chosen trousers say just as much.

Ekitike is a French striker known for his pace, movement, and finishing. The way he carries himself, on the pitch and off it, is part of the story this page tells.

04 · Career Snapshot

The Road So Far

  • 2002

    Born in Reims, France

    Came up through the youth system at his hometown club, Stade de Reims.

  • 2021

    Loan to Vejle Boldklub

    Spent a season in Denmark, scoring 3 goals in 11 appearances.

  • 2022–23

    Paris Saint-Germain

    Joined on loan and played alongside Messi, Neymar, and Mbappé. He won Ligue 1 in his first season in Paris.

  • 2024

    Eintracht Frankfurt

    Moved to Frankfurt, where he found regular minutes and his career took off.

  • 2024–25

    Bundesliga breakout

    Scored 15 Bundesliga goals and provided 8 assists, earning a place in the Bundesliga Team of the Season.

  • 2025

    Liverpool

    Signed from Frankfurt in July 2025 and scored on his Liverpool debut in the Community Shield.

  • 2026

    Achilles injury and recovery

    Ruptured his Achilles against PSG in April 2026 and had surgery soon after. He is now in rehabilitation, and his return date has not been set.

06 · Style Notes

Confidence is the real accessory. Editor's note

A strong look starts with fit. Ekitike favors pieces that move with him, from relaxed trousers to structured jackets, and he isn't afraid of bold texture or color.

The result is a style that feels natural rather than staged, which is exactly what makes it work.
